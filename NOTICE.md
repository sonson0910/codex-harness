# Publication scope

Published: the locally developed evidence validator, synthetic regression tests,
and public-edition Lean workflow documentation. Excluded: third-party skill sources,
plugin bundles, machine configuration, private acceptance plans and live evidence.

The workflow incorporates common engineering ideas such as small changes, explicit
ownership, testable acceptance and independent risk-based review. No third-party
product source or redistribution rights are granted by this repository.
